How to use

Part dry hair into sections. Apply a small amount to the scalp and hair, then massage gently for 5–10 minutes. Leave for 4–5 hours or overnight before washing with shampoo. Use 2–3 times weekly, following the product label.

Patch test before first use. Avoid contact with eyes. Discontinue if irritation occurs. Ingredient and certification claims should be confirmed from the final packaging.
