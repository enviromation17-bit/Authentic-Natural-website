A LITTLE TIME FOR YOUR HAIR

Your routine,
one step at a time.

Follow these six simple steps. Each picture includes English and Urdu instructions.

STEP 1 OF 6

Dispense into your palm

Shake well, then dispense a small amount into your palm.

STEP 2 OF 6

Apply to scalp and hair

Use clean fingertips to spread the oil evenly across your scalp and hair.

STEP 3 OF 6

Massage gently

Massage gently with your fingertips for 5 to 10 minutes.

STEP 4 OF 6

Leave it to nourish

Leave the oil on for 4 to 5 hours, or overnight if comfortable.

STEP 5 OF 6

Wrap in a warm towel

Before washing, wrap your hair in a warm steamed towel for up to one hour.

STEP 6 OF 6

Wash thoroughly

Wash your hair as you normally would. Use three times a week.

Step 1 of 6

Care and storage

For external use only. Avoid contact with eyes. Keep out of reach of children. Store below 30°C.
